Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Last updated 8 October 2026 · Terms of Use

PetLoop is an app for pet owners: your pets' records, care and health, a community of owners, a vet finder and shared deals. This policy explains what information the app keeps, why, who can see it and what you can do about it. It is written to describe what the app actually does; when the app changes, this page changes with it.

1. Who is responsible

PetLoop is operated by [company name] ("we"). For anything about your information write to [contact email].

2. What we collect and why

WhatExamplesWhy

3. Who can see your information

  • Only you: your pets, their care and health records, documents, budget and basket, and the deals you saved. Nobody else in the community can open them.
  • Other signed-in members: what you publish in the community (posts, comments, likes, photos), your member profile, and the deals you share. Chat messages are visible to the people in that chat.
  • Moderators: content that was reported and the messages around it, so they can decide on the report.
  • Service providers that work for us:
    • Supabase hosts the database, file storage and sign-in (servers in [region]).
    • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging delivers push notifications when you turn them on; it receives your device token and the notification text.
    • Google Places answers vet searches that go beyond our own directory; it receives the search location.
    • Google Fonts serves the app's typeface; Google's servers see your device's IP address when the font loads.
  • Authorities: when the law requires it, or to protect someone from serious harm, and only what is needed.

4. How we protect it

Everything travels encrypted (TLS). Each account's rows in the database are locked to that account by row-level security, files live in private storage buckets, and photos and documents are served through short-lived links. Access by administrators is logged.

5. How long we keep it

  • Your account and everything in it: for as long as the account exists.
  • When you delete your account: your pets, records, files, community content and chat messages are deleted; stored files are removed by a clean-up job within a few days. Crash reports lose the link to your account and are deleted within 90 days.
  • Moderation records (what was reported, what was decided) may be kept as long as needed to keep the community safe and to meet legal obligations.
  • Find a vet searches are not kept.

6. Your rights

Under Israel's Protection of Privacy Law, 5741-1981, you may ask to see the information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. In the app you can edit almost everything yourself and delete your account from the account menu. For anything else, write to [contact email]. You may also complain to the Privacy Protection Authority.

You control the optional parts: push notifications and location access can be turned off in the app or the phone's settings at any time.

7. Children

PetLoop is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly keep information about children; if you believe a child has an account, write to us and we will remove it.

8. Changes

When this policy changes in a way that matters, the app tells you before the change applies. The version and date at the top say which edition you are reading.
