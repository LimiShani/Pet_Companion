Design proposal

Open the mockup in its own tab
To approve or ask for changes: tell the lead in the chat, for example "approve health" or "store: drop the share-a-deal form". The agent is paused and builds nothing until the lead passes on your answer.

How to steer. Type a note on an agent's card to change its scope or design. The agent reads its notes before each milestone and answers here. For anything that crosses agents (shared code, merging, the database), ask the lead in the chat.

What you are looking at. The step and the ticked milestones are what the agent reports. Commits, files changed and uncommitted files come straight from git, so they cannot drift from reality.
